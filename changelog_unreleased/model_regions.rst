* Added options for model regions to ``ISO3``: ``AR5`` adds the RC5 and RC10 regions of
  the IPCC Fifth Assessment Report, Working Group III (``AR5|NAM``, ``AR5|OECD-1990``,
  …), ``RCP`` the 5 and 10 regions of the RCP Database (``RCP|OECD90``,
  ``RCP|R10NAM``, …), and ``EDGAR_HYDE`` the 13 regions of the EDGAR-HYDE historical
  emissions data set (``EDGAR-HYDE|Africa``, …). They follow the sources, which do not
  assign all countries to a region.
* Added the option ``PRIMAP1_inputs`` and the alias ``ISO3_PRIMAP1_INPUTS``, which add
  the codes of the input data of PRIMAP-hist v2.x as alternative codes, like ``R5OECD``
  for ``RCP|OECD90`` and ``FSU`` for ``EDGAR-HYDE|CIS``.

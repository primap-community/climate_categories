* ISO3: Added the parties to the UNFCCC over time. For every month in which parties
  joined or left the UNFCCC, there is a category ``UNFCCC_YYYY_MM`` containing the
  parties after all changes in that month took effect, based on the dates on which the
  Convention entered into force for a party or its withdrawal took effect. The comments
  give the exact period during which each category describes the parties. The last
  category of every year is also available as ``UNFCCC_YYYY``, so that for example
  ``ISO3["UNFCCC_2022"]`` gives the parties after the Holy See joined on 2022-10-04.
  Only changes which took effect before the data was last updated are included, and
  codes for the current month and year are left out, so that all dated codes are
  stable. For example, the withdrawal of the USA taking effect on 2027-02-27 is not
  included yet.
